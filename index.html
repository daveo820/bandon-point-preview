01 The Property

A secluded coastal retreat, minutes from Bandon Dunes

Nestled among grassy dunes and mature forest on six private acres overlooking the Pacific Ocean, Bandon Point is a secluded coastal retreat just minutes from Bandon Dunes. Designed by the architects behind Ghost Tree Grill and the Lodge at Old Macdonald, Bandon Point will combine modern Pacific Northwest architecture, breathtaking scenery, and an unparalleled location as the only luxury vacation rental that is both close to the resort and located directly on the coast.

02 The Residence

Designed for gatherings of close friends

With gatherings of close friends in mind, the residence will feature four king bedrooms with private baths, a gourmet kitchen, and expansive walls of glass framing panoramic ocean views. The layout will be ideally suited for four golfers, each with a private bedroom and bath, yet equally inviting for couples, whether everyone plays golf or not.

03 The Setting

Plenty of reasons to stay put

For those spending a day away from the courses, the setting will offer plenty of reasons to stay put. Walk along the beach, unwind in the oceanfront hot tub or sauna, enjoy the ever-changing coastal scenery, or settle in beside the wood-burning fireplace with a good book. With no neighboring homes in sight, Bandon Point will offer a rare sense of privacy and connection to its spectacular natural surroundings.

An invitation

We invite you to experience Bandon Point for yourself—a place where good people, a great game, and the beauty of the Oregon Coast come together to create memories you’ll never forget.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Still curious? Email info@bandonpoint.com.

Where is Bandon Point?

Bandon Point sits on six private acres overlooking the Pacific Ocean on the Oregon Coast, nestled among grassy dunes and mature forest just minutes from Bandon Dunes.

When will Bandon Point open?

Bandon Point is expected to open in summer 2028. The residence is not yet built, and dates for stays have not been announced.

Can I book a stay at Bandon Point yet?

Not yet. Bandon Point is still in development and is not yet available to book. Join the list or email info@bandonpoint.com to hear first when that changes.

How close is Bandon Point to Bandon Dunes?

Bandon Point is just minutes from Bandon Dunes. It will be the only luxury vacation rental that is both close to the resort and located directly on the coast.

How many bedrooms will Bandon Point have?

The residence will have four king bedrooms, each with its own private bath.

Is Bandon Point a good fit for a golf group?

Yes. The layout is ideally suited for four golfers, each with a private bedroom and bath.

Do we all need to play golf?

No. Bandon Point is equally inviting for couples, whether everyone plays golf or not. On days away from the courses, guests can walk the beach, unwind in the oceanfront hot tub or sauna, or settle in beside the wood-burning fireplace.

Who designed Bandon Point?

Bandon Point is designed by the architects behind Ghost Tree Grill and the Lodge at Old Macdonald, in a modern Pacific Northwest style.

What amenities will the home include?

Plans include four king bedrooms with private baths, a gourmet kitchen, expansive walls of glass framing panoramic ocean views, an oceanfront hot tub, a sauna, and a wood-burning fireplace.

How private is the property?

Very. Bandon Point occupies six private acres with no neighboring homes in sight.
