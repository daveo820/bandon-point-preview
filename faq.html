What is Bandon Point?

Bandon Point is a secluded coastal retreat in development on six private acres overlooking the Pacific Ocean, just minutes from Bandon Dunes on the Oregon Coast. It is expected to open in summer 2028; it is not yet built and is not yet available to book.

Where is Bandon Point?

Nestled among grassy dunes and mature trees on six private acres overlooking the Pacific Ocean, Bandon Point is a secluded coastal retreat just minutes from Bandon Dunes.

When will Bandon Point open?

Bandon Point is expected to open in summer 2028. The residence is not yet built, and dates for stays have not been announced.

Can I book a stay at Bandon Point yet?

Not yet. Bandon Point is still in development and is not yet available to book. Join the list or email info@bandonpoint.com to hear first when that changes.

How close is Bandon Point to Bandon Dunes?

Bandon Point is just minutes from Bandon Dunes. It will be the only vacation rental of its kind that is both close to the resort and located directly on the ocean.

How many bedrooms will Bandon Point have?

The residence will have four king bedrooms, each with a private bath and ocean view.

Is Bandon Point a good fit for a golf group?

Yes. The layout will be ideally suited for four golfers, with four king bedrooms with private baths.

Do we all need to play golf?

No. Bandon Point is equally inviting for couples, whether everyone plays golf or not. Walk along the beach, explore nearby mountain biking trails, or browse the shops and galleries in Old Town Bandon. Back at the residence, unwind in the oceanfront hot tub or sauna, or settle in beside the wood-burning fireplace.

Who designed Bandon Point?

Bandon Point is designed by the architects behind Ghost Tree Grill, Round Lake Lodge, and the Lodge at Old Macdonald, and will bring together modern Pacific Northwest architecture, breathtaking scenery, and an extraordinary oceanfront setting just minutes from Bandon Dunes.

What amenities will the home include?

The residence will feature four king bedrooms with private baths, a gourmet kitchen, and expansive walls of glass framing panoramic ocean views, plus an oceanfront hot tub, a sauna, and a wood-burning fireplace.

How private is the property?

Very. Bandon Point occupies six private acres with 180-degree views of the Pacific and not another home in sight.

Is Bandon Point a vacation rental near Bandon Dunes?

Yes. Bandon Point is a secluded coastal retreat just minutes from Bandon Dunes, and it will be the only vacation rental of its kind that is both close to the resort and located directly on the ocean. It is expected to open in summer 2028.

Is Bandon Point an oceanfront home?

Yes. Bandon Point sits on six private acres overlooking the Pacific, with an oceanfront hot tub and expansive walls of glass framing panoramic ocean views. Guests can walk along the beach.

What size group is Bandon Point best for?

Bandon Point has four king bedrooms with private baths. The layout will be ideally suited for four golfers, yet equally inviting for couples, whether everyone plays golf or not.

Is Bandon Point part of Bandon Dunes Golf Resort?

No. Bandon Point is a privately owned property near Bandon Dunes Golf Resort.

What architectural style will Bandon Point be?

Modern Pacific Northwest architecture, designed by the architects behind Ghost Tree Grill, Round Lake Lodge, and the Lodge at Old Macdonald.

Which golf courses are near Bandon Point?

Bandon Dunes Golf Resort has five 18 hole courses (Bandon Dunes, Pacific Dunes, Bandon Trails, Old Macdonald and Sheep Ranch), two par 3 courses (Bandon Preserve and Shorty's) and the Punchbowl putting course. Bandon Crossings is an independent 18 hole course south of Bandon.

How do I book tee times near Bandon Point?

Tee times are booked directly with each course.

How far is Bandon Point from each course?

Bandon Point is just minutes from Bandon Dunes. Approximate drive times to each course will be published once the property address is confirmed.
