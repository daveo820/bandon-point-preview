A coastal home base for your Bandon Dunes golf trip

Coming Summer 2028: four king bedrooms with private baths on six private acres overlooking the Pacific, just minutes from the resort.

Close to the Resort, On the Coast

Stay on the ocean, minutes from the first tee

Bandon Point will be the only vacation rental of its kind that is both close to the resort and located directly on the ocean.

Designed by the architects behind Ghost Tree Grill, Round Lake Lodge, and the Lodge at Old Macdonald, Bandon Point will combine modern Pacific Northwest architecture, breathtaking scenery, and an unparalleled location.

Built for a Foursome

A private bedroom and bath for every golfer

With gatherings of close friends in mind, the residence will feature four king bedrooms with private baths, a gourmet kitchen, and expansive walls of glass framing panoramic ocean views. The layout will be ideally suited for four golfers, yet equally inviting for couples, whether everyone plays golf or not.

Between Rounds

For the days away from the courses

For those spending a day away from the course, the setting offers plenty of reasons to stay put. Walk along the beach, unwind in the oceanfront hot tub or sauna, or simply relax beside the wood-burning fireplace and soak up the sights and sounds of the Pacific, all with no cars or homes in sight.

Golf Trip Questions

Planning ahead

Where is Bandon Point?

Nestled among grassy dunes and mature trees on six private acres overlooking the Pacific, Bandon Point is a secluded coastal retreat just minutes from Bandon Dunes.

When will Bandon Point open?

Bandon Point is expected to open in summer 2028. The residence is not yet built, and dates for stays have not been announced.

Can I book a stay at Bandon Point yet?

Not yet. Bandon Point is still in development and is not yet available to book. Join the list or email info@bandonpoint.com to hear first when that changes.

How close is Bandon Point to Bandon Dunes?

Bandon Point is just minutes from Bandon Dunes. It will be the only vacation rental of its kind that is both close to the resort and located directly on the ocean.

Is Bandon Point a good fit for a golf group?

Yes. The layout will be ideally suited for four golfers, with four king bedrooms with private baths.

Do we all need to play golf?

No. Bandon Point is equally inviting for couples, whether everyone plays golf or not. Walk along the beach, unwind in the oceanfront hot tub or sauna, or simply relax beside the wood-burning fireplace and soak up the sights and sounds of the Pacific.
